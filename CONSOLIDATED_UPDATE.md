# Consolidated Math Advisor update

## K232 and K342 offline practice
- K232 Additional Mathematics is enabled for deterministic offline practice.
- K342 Additional Mathematics is added as a selectable 2027 SEC track.
- Topics span Algebra, Geometry & Trigonometry and Calculus.
- Foundation, Similar and Stretch select different question families and cognitive demands.

## Worksheet assessment type
- Total marks and duration are hidden in Worksheet mode.
- The generator receives no total-mark or duration target for a worksheet.
- Teacher worked answers can still be generated.
- Worksheets are not intended to fail simply because marks do not reconcile.

## Student working + GeoGebra
- Students can open GeoGebra Graphing or GeoGebra Geometry from the Student working workflow.
- GeoGebra opens separately; uploaded student images are not automatically transferred.

## 3D object diagrams
Generated 3D questions target the supplied examination-diagram style:
- black wireframe on white;
- clearly labelled vertices;
- dimensions beside the corresponding edges;
- mathematically consistent perspective;
- structured vertices/edges preferred for cuboids, prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones and composite solids.

The supplied image is used as a style reference rather than copied into unrelated questions.
